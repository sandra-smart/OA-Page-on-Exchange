About the OA

Objectives

The Opportunity Accelerator is designed to accomplish four objectives.

🎯
Equip Job Seekers

Provide access to resources needed to obtain employment, advance careers, and experience economic mobility.

🤝
Engage Teams & Partners

Connect Goodwill team members and partners with best-in-class resources to enhance workforce development services.

🌐
Connect Communities

Collaborate with national partners to connect Goodwill members and their communities to resources and support.

📣
Advocate & Promote

Engage in advocacy and public relations to educate communities and promote the success of the OA model.

Organizational Preparation

The tools, resources and training in the Organizational Preparation step of the Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® supports mission teams as you train your staff, align on systems and processes, gain internal stakeholder buy-in, and build community and employer partnerships.

Org Prep · 1

Functionally Aligned Staffing Model

The organization must demonstrate through its staffing model that core functions are aligned with OA best practices — staff are organized into distinct functional groups, each covering a specific area of OA service delivery.

Gold: Core functions covered Platinum: All 5 roles required
Mission Playbook Chapter 2 - Staffing Your Mission Strategy → Staffing Structures Case Study →
Org Prep · 2

Goodwill Career Coach & Navigator Certificate

Goodwill® Career Navigator and Coach Professional Certificate for team members providing coaching and career navigation services, which includes intensive, collaborative support to help clients identify and address employment barriers.

Gold: 50% certified in Year 1 Platinum: 100% certified in Year 1
Professional Certificate License Request → 🔧 In development- Professional Certificate Flyer
Org Prep · 3

OA Fellows Program

Leadership development through participation in the OA Fellows program. OA Fellows are ambassadors for the OA initiative, trained to understand the framework and equip Goodwill peers to deploy it locally.

Gold: At least 1 Fellow Platinum: At least 2 Fellows
OA Fellows Application →
Org Prep · 4

Referral & Wraparound Network Integration

Building partnerships and using referral technology to connect individuals to support services.

Gold: Referral process + partnerships Platinum: ART platform required
🔧 In development- ART Tools on Exchange
Org Prep · 5

Employer Partnership Planning & Outreach

Engaging employers using labor market data to co-create training and job placement strategies.

Gold: Local employer referral partnerships Platinum: LMI-informed, co-created training
🔧 In development- Employer Engagement Toolkit
Org Prep · 6

Evaluating Impact & Metrics

Collecting and analyzing data to measure KPIs and participant feedback throughout the journey.

Gold: Short KPI list, 180-day follow-up Platinum: Full KPI list, 365-day follow-up
🔧 In development- CaseWorthy on Exchange

Phase 1 — Assess & Plan

The tools, resources and training in the Assess and Plan phase of the Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® supports Goodwill career coaches and navigators as they recruit job seekers, conduct intake and assessments, create career and financial plans, and collect and report intake and enrollment data.

Phase 1 · 7

Outreach & Recruitment

Promote mission services using diverse outreach methods and referral technologies.

Gold: Not required Platinum: ART + referral partners
🔧 In development- ART Tools on Exchange
Phase 1 · 8

Registration & Intake

Conduct intake services to meet with potential clients/job seekers, learn more about who they are, and collect demographic, work and educational history, and other personal information that determines eligibility.

Gold: Short KPI list Platinum: Long KPI list
🔧 In development- Updated Pre-Enrollment Form
Phase 1 · 9a

Needs Assessment

Holistic evaluation of barriers across financial capability, healthcare, food security, housing, transportation, legal issues, and family care.

Gold: Valid Assessments Required Platinum: Valid Assessments Required
🔧 In development- Recommended Assessment Tool
Phase 1 · 9b

Basic Education Assessment

Measure reading, writing, math, and language proficiency using validated tools (TABE, CASAS, TOEFL, or similar). Required for participants without a diploma who are enrolling in occupational training — prevents placement in programs where literacy requirements exceed current skill levels.

Gold: For non-diploma enrollees Platinum: For non-diploma enrollees
🔧 In development- Recommended Assessment Tool
Phase 1 · 9c

Career Readiness & Interest Assessment

Evaluate work-related interests, soft skills, and career readiness using validated tools like JOFI or the O*NET Interest Profiler. Helps Career Navigators match participants to career pathways that align with both their interests and the local labor market.

Gold: Valid assessment required Platinum: Valid assessment required
O*NET Interest Profiler →
Phase 1 · 9d

Digital Skills Assessment

Measure digital literacy baseline using Northstar (required at Platinum) or another valid tool. Results guide decisions about which level of digital skills training to provide — from basic computer use through industry-specific certifications.

Gold: Any valid assessment Platinum: Northstar required
Northstar Assessment →
Phase 1 · 10

Orientation

The official start of the participant's OA journey. Every participant must complete orientation before enrollment at both levels. Participants learn what services are available, what's expected of them, and how to navigate their pathway. Informed commitment — not passive sign-up.

Gold: Orientation is built into the onboarding process Platinum: Orientation is built into the onboarding process
🔧 In development- Orientation Resource
Phase 1 · 11

Individual Career & Financial Plan (ICFP)

The personalized roadmap where everything comes together. Every participant completes an ICFP with SMART goals covering their career pathway, financial stability, and self-sufficiency. This living document guides every service decision and is updated as participants progress.

Gold: Participants develop an Individual Career and Financial Plan in partnership with a career navigator Platinum: Participants develop an Individual Career and Financial Plan in partnership with a career navigator
🔧 In development- Updated IFCP Form
🛠
Phase 2 — Equip & Connect

The tools, resources and training in the Equip and Connect phase of the Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® supports Goodwill career coaches and navigators with implementing skills training programs, providing holistic support services for program participants, and preparing job seekers for employment in the community.

Phase 2 · 12

Wraparound Supports

Provide support services to remove barriers to success including remedial education, financial capability, health care, mental health, food security, housing stability, transportation, legal issues, family care (adult and child), and clothing needed for employment.

Gold: Resources aligned with individual needs Platinum: ART-coordinated referrals
🔧 In development- ART Tools on Exchange
Phase 2 · 13

Financial Education

Guidance on personal finance management and planning.

Gold: Must offer to 100% Platinum: Must offer to 100%
🔧 In development- Financial Education Resource
Phase 2 · 14

Foundational Skills Training

Training on personal and interpersonal competencies for employment.

Gold: Must offer foundational training Platinum: Skills to Succeed Academy required
Skills to Succeed Academy →
Phase 2 · 15

Digital Skills Training

Teach use of computers, software, and online tools for employment.

Gold: GDCA framework, needs-based Platinum: GDCA + Northstar Online Learning
🔧 In development- Digital Skills Training
Phase 2 · 16

Occupation-Specific Skills Training

Training and certifications leading to industry-recognized credentials.

Gold: Aligned with local labor market Platinum: Living wage focus + employer co-creation
Link TBD — GCTA, Cisco, IBM, Coursera & more
Phase 2 · 17

Career Exploration Opportunities

Hands-on experiences such as job shadowing, OJT, learn-and-earn, internships, pre-apprenticeships, apprenticeships, job site tours, informational employment observations, and simulated environments using VR headsets.

Gold: Not required Platinum: Must offer career exploration
🔧 In development- Career Exploration Opportunities
Phase 2 · 18

Job Search & Prep Assistance

Support with resumes, interviews, and job applications.

Gold: Résumé + interview prep required Platinum: Résumé + interview prep required
🔧 In development- Job Search and Prep Assistance
🚀
Phase 3 — Launch & Evaluate

The tools, resources and training in the Launch and Evaluate phase of the Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® supports Goodwill career coaches and navigators with enhancing job placement efforts, supporting job retention and career progression, and optimizing post-program alumni engagement.

Phase 3 · 19

Job Placement Assistance

Engage employers to identify job openings and create employment opportunities.

Gold: Must offer placement assistance Platinum: Living wage placements required
🔧 In development- Job Placement Assistance
Phase 3 · 20

Retention Services & Follow-Up

Ongoing support after job placement or training completion to help individuals retain employment and advance in their careers, including access to wraparound supports and skill-building aligned with their goals.

Gold: Follow-up at 30/60/90/180 days Platinum: Follow-up through 365 days
🔧 In development- Retention and Follow-Up
Phase 3 · 21

Alumni Engagement

Long-term engagement of individuals after program completion or job placement — extending beyond one year, through networking, mentorship, and/or leveraging alumni as teachers or teaching assistants.

Gold: Not required Platinum: At least 1 engagement method
🔧 In development- Alumni Engagement
